## 2.4.0d

A patch that adds **Ava Day** (Friend Day) - an alta holiday celebrating friendship and connection! This update also introduces Alta Tailor crafting station and a new level-dependant prefix and parameter system for equipment and food!

**Main:**

- added 2 food items;
- added 4 decorations;
- added 3 NPCs and tenants;
- added 1 secret merchant NPC;
- added 1 crafting station;
- added 2 codex entries;
- added 1 toy;
- numerous bug, typo and tag fixes.

**Ava Day:**

- added **Ava Day** event content celebrating friendship and all types of bonds:
  - **Friendship Candy** - small wrapped plant-based chocolate for gifting, comes in 4 color variants;
  - **Ava Cookie** (perfect version - **Perfect Ava Cookie**) - heart-shaped cookie with gheatsyn sparkle;
  - **Alta Love Poster** - festive poster celebrating friendship;
  - **Ava Day Poster** - festive poster celebrating friendship;
  - **Ava Day Gift Box** - wrapped gift with crystalline bow;
  - **Ava Day Gift Package** - alternative gift package design;
  - **Alta Cupid** - a cupid-themed tenant who loves matchmaking and spreading love;
  - **Alta Love Merchant** - a secret merchant who sells event-exclusive items;
  - **Alta Girlfriend** - a tenant who celebrates love and companionship.

![ ](/.meta/images/showcase/2.4.0d/ava_day.png)

**Alta:**

- added **Alta Tailor** crafting station:
  - allows crafting clothing, plushies, dyes and other tailoring-related items and objects;
  - added 2 new material recipes:
    - **Fabric** - crafted from plant fibre and ionic sap;
    - **Cotton Wool** - crafted from cotton and ionic sap.
- added **Ceterai Helper** NPC - a specialized merchant with unique colorful equipment.

**Translation:**

- updated numerous species descriptions;
- added Ava Day content in `/items/generic/food/events/ava_day/`, `/objects/alta/special/events/ava_day/` and `/items/active/alta/toys/bows/`;
- renamed android spawners to android nades;
- other typo fixes.

**Dev:**

- improved item build script (`items/buildscripts/alta/item.lua`):
  - cleaned up bonus calculation logic;
  - streamlined preset parameter handling;
  - added support for `cfg` parameter for direct config merging;
  - improved `levelShop` parameter support;
  - fixed color index handling for table inputs;
  - optimized base asset merging.
- reorganized recipe groups:
  - all plushie recipes now include `alta_tailor` group;
  - all dye recipes now include `alta_tailor` group;
  - all vanilla dyes craftable at Alta Tailor;
  - all vanilla cloth materials craftable at Alta Tailor.
- updated treasure pools:
  - added **Alta Impulse Rifle** (level 6) to tier 6 pods with 0.01 weight;
  - rebalanced **Plasmora** weight from 0.02 to 0.01.
- enhanced dungeon system with ayaka-themed parkour and bridge structures.

**Other:**

- fixed tags on a lot of items and objects;
- fixed clothing recipes only requiring 2 silk;
- minor bug fixes.

## 2.4.0a

A patch that fixes a progression loop with dendrarium codex entries, and brings support for the Growing Trays mod.

**Main:**

- added support for Growing Trays:
  - alta fertilizers can now be used with the mod and have a low chance of being consumed and high fertilizing value;

## 2.4.0

An update that brings you the Dendrarium - a farming & botanics station with new and improved tools, saplings, flowers, fertilizers and more!

Crafted at the tier 1 **Alta Crafting Station**, you can use it to craft farming tools, discover and upgrade saplings, pot 20+ flowers and plants and craft other botanics-related items, including extracted 4 crop seeds, 8 grass seeds, 6 flower pots, 6 eco pods, 40+ upgraded crop/tree saplings, 2 plant-like critters, 5 tillers, 5 watering cans, 6 sprinklers, 7 wood cutters, 10 fertilizers and 10 codex entries.

Some of the tools are upgradeable at the **Weapon Upgrade Anvil**.
