## 1.0.0

Welcome to my mod! Hope you'll enjoy it as much as I do! ^^

- added a set of armor - the [C.T. Set](https://ceterai.github.io/MyEnternia/Wiki/C.T.-Set), including chest, legs and 2 helmets with unique abilities;
- added 2 upgradeable one-handed guns - [Fixed Point SMG](https://ceterai.github.io/MyEnternia/Wiki/EDSMNG) and [Fixed Point Hyperblaster](https://ceterai.github.io/MyEnternia/Wiki/DoublePointMNG);
- added 2 crafting materials - [Cetereye](https://ceterai.github.io/MyEnternia/Wiki/Cetereye) and [Ceter-Sphere](https://ceterai.github.io/MyEnternia/Wiki/CeterSphere), that are needed to craft most of the mod items;
- these materials can be purchased at [Treasured Trophies](https://starbounder.org/Treasured_Trophies) or in [Tabula Rasa](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=737353165) if installed.
