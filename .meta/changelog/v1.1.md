## 1.1.0

This is a general expansion that adds a planet type and some content to it.

- desided to give the mod a proper name;
- added new planet type - [Enternia](https://ceterai.github.io/MyEnternia/Wiki/Enternia);
- added 1 new biome with a similar name;
- added a tree type for the new biome;
- added food and crops - [Aya](https://ceterai.github.io/MyEnternia/Wiki/Aya), [Gil](https://ceterai.github.io/MyEnternia/Wiki/Gil) and [Tsay](https://ceterai.github.io/MyEnternia/Wiki/Tsay), as well as prepared food made from them;
- added 1 upgradeable sword.
